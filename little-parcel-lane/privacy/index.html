Privacy Policy

Little Parcel Lane · Effective 22 October 2026

Little Parcel Lane is a cozy puzzle game published by Stardust Pixel. The current version of the game does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no chat and no in-app purchases.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress, coins, boosters and settings (music, sound, vibration) are saved only on your device. We have no server and never receive them. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes them.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your own Google account's device backup. That is your backup, under Google's terms; we cannot see it.

Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, for haptic feedback. It does not ask for internet access and does not send any information about you anywhere.

Children

The game has no violent or adult content, but it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; the current version collects none from anyone.

Google Play

When you download or update the game, Google Play handles that under Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Changes and contact

We do not sell any data. Later versions are planned to add ads (Google AdMob) and in-app purchases through Google Play. Before any such version is released, this page will describe exactly what those services collect, ask for consent where the law requires it, and carry a new date; the Play Store listing will change at the same time.
