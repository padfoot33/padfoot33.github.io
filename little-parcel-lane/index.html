Little Parcel Lane

by Stardust Pixel

Every cozy room hides the orders your neighbours are waiting for. Spot three matching items, tap them into your tray and pack each parcel before the tray fills up.

  • 30 hand-painted rooms: a bakery windmill, a lighthouse, a candy shop, a snowy cabin and more
  • Friendly residents who hop, cheer and react as you pack
  • Calm, relaxing puzzles with gentle challenges as you go
  • Daily deliveries, the Stamp Crate and the Delivery Route reward you for coming back
